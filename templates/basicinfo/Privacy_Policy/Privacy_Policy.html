Return & Refund Policy (Strict Guidelines)

IMPORTANT: Returns/refunds for wrong or defective products are ONLY accepted when you provide unboxing video evidence showing the product issue clearly.

Our Return Policy in Detail

  1. Video Evidence Mandatory

    To process any return for wrong/defective items, you must provide a continuous, unedited video showing:

    • The sealed package before opening
    • The unboxing process from start to finish
    • Clear visibility of the product defect/wrong item
    • All original packaging and labels

    Videos must be submitted within 24 hours of delivery.

  2. Eligibility Conditions

    We accept returns ONLY when:

    • Product is defective (with video proof)
    • Wrong item received (with video proof)
    • Package is tampered/damaged (with video proof)

    No returns accepted for buyer's remorse or size/color preference changes.

  3. Non-Returnable Items

    These can't be returned under any circumstances:

    • Personal care items (earrings, cosmetics, etc.)
    • Digital products/downloads
    • Gift cards/vouchers
    • Customized/personalized items
  4. Refund Process

    If your return is approved:

    • Refunds processed within 7-10 business days
    • Original payment method will be credited
    • Shipping charges non-refundable unless our error
Note: Any return request without proper video evidence will be automatically rejected. This policy helps protect both customers and our business from fraudulent claims.
