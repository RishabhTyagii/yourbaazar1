Delivery FAQs

Answers to common questions about our delivery services

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary based on your location and the shipping method you choose. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days, while express delivery reaches you in 1-2 business days. Metro cities typically have faster delivery times.

Can I track my order?

Yes! Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can track your package in real-time through our website or the courier company's tracking system.

Do you deliver on weekends?

We deliver on Saturdays in most locations. Sunday delivery is available in select metro cities for express shipments. You'll be notified about delivery days when your order ships.

What are your delivery charges?

Delivery charges vary based on order value, weight, and destination. We offer free standard shipping on orders over ₹999. Exact shipping costs are calculated at checkout before you complete your purchase.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Address changes are possible if your order hasn't been shipped yet. Contact our customer support immediately with your order number and new address. Once shipped, we cannot guarantee address changes.
