Our Story

From a college project to a thriving marketplace - the journey of YourBaazar

Founded in 2021 by Rishabh Tyagi and Tarun Tyagi, two visionary engineering students, YourBaazar was born out of a simple idea: to create a platform that makes buying and reselling products effortless for everyone.

What started as a dorm room discussion quickly evolved into a full-fledged e-commerce platform. The brothers combined their technical skills with entrepreneurial spirit to build a marketplace that bridges the gap between buyers looking for great deals and sellers wanting to reach more customers.

Today, YourBaazar has grown into a trusted name in the resale market, serving thousands of users across India with a seamless shopping experience.
