Seller Policies

Clear guidelines to help you succeed on our platform

General Terms & Onboarding

  • By registering as a seller on the YourBaazar platform, you agree to comply with all policies, rules, and obligations stated herein.
  • Seller must provide accurate legal documents, business information, GST details, bank account details, and valid identity/address proof.
  • YourBaazar reserves the right to review, approve, or reject seller applications at its sole discretion.
  • If uploaded documents are unsatisfactory, incomplete, forged, or unverifiable, YourBaazar may request re-upload, temporary suspension, or permanent blocking of the seller account.
  • Seller approval is mandatory before any product listing goes live.
  • Approval/rejection of documents shall be communicated via registered email/YourBaazar dashboard.
  • YourBaazar reserves the right to perform KYC re-verification anytime.
  • Sellers must comply with all applicable laws including GST, Consumer Protection Act, IT Act, and e-commerce regulations in India.
  • By accepting this agreement, the seller authorizes YourBaazar to collect, store, and process data related to sales, settlements, logistics, and disputes.
  • Any violation of this policy may result in suspension, withholding of settlements, or permanent termination.

Product Listing & Approval

  • Sellers must submit product details, images, pricing, and shipping details for approval.
  • Every new product request must be submitted through the seller dashboard.
  • YourBaazar will review the product request within 24 hours.
  • Upon approval, the product will be listed on YourBaazar marketplace within the next 24 hours.
  • Products found misleading, fake, counterfeit, or violating intellectual property rights will be rejected.
  • Banned items such as illegal products, narcotics, weapons, hazardous chemicals, expired goods, or items restricted under law are strictly prohibited.
  • Seller must maintain accurate stock availability in the system.
  • Any discrepancy in stock may result in order cancellation and penalties.
  • Sellers must ensure high-quality product images with clear descriptions.
  • Product pricing must include applicable GST and comply with MRP regulations.

Shipping & Delivery

  • YourBaazar follows a Hybrid Shipping Model where shipping charges are partly paid by customers and partly borne by sellers.
  • Each product will have a Base Shipping Price defined by the seller.
  • The base shipping price will be collected from the customer at checkout.
  • If the actual shipping cost exceeds the base price, the excess amount shall be borne by the seller.
  • The same base shipping price applies to multiple quantities of the same product.
  • If a customer orders more than one unit of the same product, shipping is charged only once (until free shipping threshold is met).
  • Free Shipping Threshold: If the order value is ₹1500 or above, the entire shipping cost will be free for the customer.
  • In such cases, YourBaazar will recover the applicable shipping charges directly from the seller.
  • Sellers are responsible for securely packing orders in accordance with YourBaazar guidelines.
  • Every package must carry YourBaazar-branded packaging/taping.

Payment & Settlements

  • Payments for successful orders will be credited to the seller's YourBaazar Wallet.
  • Wallet settlement cycle operates weekly.
  • Orders delivered and accepted till Friday night will be added for settlement.
  • Settlement processing is carried out on Saturday and Sunday.
  • Final transfer to the seller's registered bank account is made on Monday.
  • Sellers are responsible for providing accurate bank details.
  • Bank charges for NEFT/IMPS/RTGS will be borne by the seller.
  • In case of failed transfers due to incorrect bank details, reprocessing charges may apply.
  • Online payments received through YourBaazar are subject to standard bank/payment gateway charges, which are deducted before settlement.
  • Sellers will receive payment summaries, including deductions, in the wallet transaction history.

Returns & Refunds

  • Customers may initiate returns as per YourBaazar Return Policy.
  • Accepted reasons include damaged product, wrong product, defective item, or unmet description.
  • Returns are processed via reverse logistics by YourBaazar.
  • Seller must accept all valid returns.
  • Refusal to accept returns will result in penalties.
  • Upon successful return pickup, the product will be delivered back to the seller.
  • Refunds to customers are managed by YourBaazar directly.
  • Refund amount will be adjusted against seller settlements.
  • Settlement for returns will reflect in Seller Wallet History.
  • Sellers can track return/refund status in the dashboard.
